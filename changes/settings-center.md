# GOLDCHAT settings centre upgrade

This release replaces the single settings panel with working controls grouped by purpose.

- **Appearance:** dark, light, and device themes; chat text size; wallpaper; time format; reduced motion.
- **Notifications & sounds:** master notifications, private chats, groups, channels, status alerts, previews, and sound.
- **Data & storage:** automatic loading preferences for images, voice notes, and video; clear offline cache.
- **Privacy & security:** status and online-presence visibility, blocked accounts, linked devices, and recovery email.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** Ctrl/Cmd+K for search, Ctrl/Cmd+N for a new chat, Escape to close a panel.

Preferences are saved on the device. Settings that affect an account profile are saved through the GOLDCHAT API.
