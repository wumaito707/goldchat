# Original GOLDCHAT wallpaper assets

These four project assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool. They are loaded only when the wallpaper gallery or a selected wallpaper needs them. The remaining gallery choices are lightweight CSS artwork defined in `appearance.js`.

Files: `ocean.png`, `alpine.png`, `botanical.png`, and `petals.png`, in this folder.

Final prompts:

- **ocean.png:** Photorealistic aerial ocean wallpaper for a messaging app. Portrait 2:3 composition. Turquoise Atlantic water with delicate white surf meeting a warm peach sandy shore diagonally, soft natural sunlight, serene uncluttered details, premium travel photography. No people, no text, no logos, no borders. Standalone full bleed wallpaper.
- **alpine.png:** Photorealistic mountain wallpaper for a messaging app. Portrait 2:3 composition. Dramatic pale alpine peaks above soft morning clouds, powder blue sky and pastel rose dawn light, serene premium landscape photography. No people, no text, no logos, no borders. Standalone full bleed wallpaper.
- **botanical.png:** Photorealistic botanical wallpaper for a messaging app. Portrait 2:3 composition. Sculptural glossy green monstera leaves casting soft shadows on a muted warm pink wall, elegant soft studio daylight, tranquil minimal premium interior photography, rich leaf detail. No text, no logos, no borders. Standalone full bleed wallpaper.
- **petals.png:** Photorealistic flower wallpaper for a messaging app. Portrait 2:3 composition. Macro of delicate lavender and soft pink peony petals, translucent morning light, layered organic abstract shapes, soft dreamy elegant photography. No text, no logos, no borders. Standalone full bleed wallpaper.
