BEFORE YOU INSTALL

A few helpful notes.

Use the official link

Download only from GOLDCHAT's own website. Do not install unofficial copies shared through unknown links.

Allow permissions when needed

Notifications, camera, microphone and contact selection each require device or browser permission. You choose what to allow.

Sign in to sync conversations

Your server-stored chat history follows your account, but local drafts and appearance choices may differ across browsers and devices.

About this release

Version 1.0.1 uses the live GOLDCHAT service and needs an internet connection. The Android download is a direct APK, not a Google Play listing; there is no Apple App Store listing at this time.
