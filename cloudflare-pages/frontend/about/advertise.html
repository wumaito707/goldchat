Sponsorship FAQ

Can I pay for a campaign immediately?

Please request and agree a quote before payment. If online checkout is configured, use the sponsorship payment page only after written agreement.

Will my promotion appear inside private messages?

No. These sponsorship options concern the public GOLDCHAT information website, not private chats.

What promotions are accepted?

We review lawful business promotions with accurate claims and working links. Deceptive offers, impersonation, malware and requests for user passwords are not accepted.
