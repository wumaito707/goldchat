HOW SPONSORSHIP WORKS

From enquiry to publication.

  1. Send your campaign brief. Describe your business, placement, budget and dates.
  2. Agree the campaign. Confirm the price, duration, artwork, link and any available traffic reporting.
  3. Confirm payment details. GOLDCHAT will provide the agreed payment instructions. Once agreed, use the online payment page when checkout is available.
  4. Your promotion goes live. Approved content is published as a labelled sponsored placement for the agreed period.
Can I pay directly on this website?

Request a quote first. After agreement, use our online payment page when Paystack checkout is configured. Sending a campaign brief does not take payment.

Will my promotion appear in private chats?

No. These sponsorship options are for public information pages.

What kind of promotions are accepted?

Legitimate business promotions with accurate claims and working links are reviewed before publication. Deceptive promotions, impersonation, malware and requests for users’ passwords are not accepted.
