01 / CONVERSATIONS

Messages that do more

Send and receive messages, reply in context, react, forward, edit your own messages or delete them. Star or pin important moments, search a conversation and see typing indicators and available read receipts.

Replies & reactionsRead receiptsSearch & pins
02 / SHARING

Photos, files & voice notes

Send images, videos, audio and documents directly in chats, or record and play a voice note. Individual attachments are currently limited to 10 MB and voice recordings stop after two minutes.

Photos & videoDocumentsVoice notes
03 / MOMENTS

24-hour status updates

Share text, photos, videos or audio with your contacts. Statuses disappear after 24 hours, and visibility depends on saved contacts and the privacy settings you choose.

24-hour updatesPrivacy controls

04 / GROUPS & CHANNELS

Keep everyone in the loop

Create a group with selected GOLDCHAT members and exchange messages together. Channel owners can publish updates for their members. Owners manage membership and settings; public community discovery is not available yet.

Member groupsOwner-led channels
05 / SHARED ACTIVITIES

Polls, tasks & events

Make a group decision with a poll, track a shared task or save event details in a conversation. These are in-chat planning tools; automatic calendar reminders are not included.

Poll votesShared tasksEvents
06 / FIND YOUR PEOPLE

Contacts & QR sharing

Find users by name, save contacts and share your GOLDCHAT profile link or QR code. Supported Android browsers can select phone contacts to check for matching accounts, with your choice of which contacts to add.

User searchProfile QRContact backup

07 / PERSONALIZATION

Make GOLDCHAT feel like you

Update your display name, photo and about text. Choose gold dark or gold light appearance, larger text and reduced animation, and organize chats with pinning, archiving and muting.

ProfilesThemesAccessibility
08 / PRIVACY & ACCOUNT

Useful privacy controls

Use email verification and password recovery, manage presence and status visibility, block contacts and set disappearing-message timers. These controls do not mean messages are end-to-end encrypted.

Block usersVisibilityDisappearing messages
09 / YOUR DEVICES

Switch between screens

Open GOLDCHAT on the web or Android. Use a one-time QR sign-in flow to link another device and manage linked sessions. Online chat history is stored with your account; local drafts and preferences stay on each device.

Web & AndroidLinked sessions

BUILT IN THE OPEN

What works—and
what’s still evolving.

We want you to know what to expect as GOLDCHAT grows.

Available now

Chat, share, organize

Messages, media, statuses, selected-member groups and channels, polls, events, contacts and account controls are part of the current app.

Limited / testing

Voice & video calls

Call controls and history exist, but reliable calling between different networks still needs connectivity setup and real-device testing.

Not available yet

Future capabilities

End-to-end encryption, public community discovery, payments, premium tools and AI features are not current GOLDCHAT capabilities.

GOLDCHAT requires an internet connection. Some features depend on device/browser permissions. The current Android APK is version 1.0.1; there is no Play Store or App Store listing yet.
