ACCOUNT & ACCESS

Getting started

Why is GOLDCHAT slow to open sometimes?

Our hosting service can pause after a period of inactivity. The first visit may need extra time while the server starts. Wait briefly, then refresh. An internet connection is required.

Where is my verification email?

Check your inbox and spam folder. Email codes are valid for 60 minutes, and resend is available after 60 seconds. If delivery is delayed, check that you entered the correct email address.

How can I reset my password?

On the GOLDCHAT sign-in screen choose Forgot password, then follow the email verification steps. If you created an older account without a verified email, first use Profile & privacy → Verify email while signed in.

Can I use GOLDCHAT on a second device?

Yes. Sign in normally or use the Link device with QR option. Approve a QR sign-in only for a device you control. You can review and unlink devices in Features → Linked devices.

CHATS & PRIVACY

Conversations and sharing

Why can't my friend see my status?

Status visibility depends on saved contacts and your privacy settings. For “My contacts” visibility, both people may need to save each other. Statuses expire after 24 hours, and blocked users cannot see each other's statuses.

Can I delete or edit a sent message?

Open the message menu for edit or delete actions. Your own messages can be edited or deleted for everyone; deleting for yourself only hides your copy. There is also a conversation option for disappearing messages.

Are GOLDCHAT messages end-to-end encrypted?

No. GOLDCHAT has account and access controls, but it does not currently provide end-to-end encryption. Please keep this in mind when deciding what to share.

Why do calls sometimes fail?

Voice and video call controls are included, but connecting calls reliably across different internet networks is still being configured and tested. The messaging features do not depend on calling being available.

DEVICES & DOWNLOADS

Using GOLDCHAT anywhere

How do I turn on notifications?

In GOLDCHAT, open Features → Settings & accessibility → Phone permissions. Allow notifications. On Android, you may also need to open GOLDCHAT App info → Notifications or your Chrome site permissions.

Where can I install GOLDCHAT?

Use GOLDCHAT in a browser on a phone or computer, or download the Android APK from the Download page. On iPhone, use Safari's Add to Home Screen feature. There is currently no official Play Store or Apple App Store listing.

Is this the messaging app?

No. You are on GOLDCHAT's public information website. Choose Open GOLDCHAT when you want to sign in and send messages.
