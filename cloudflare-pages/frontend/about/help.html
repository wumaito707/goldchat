A LITTLE HELP, WHEN YOU NEED IT

Questions, answered.

Why is the first visit sometimes slow?

The free hosting server can sleep when unused. Give it time to wake up, then retry if necessary. An internet connection is required.

Where is my email verification code?

Check your inbox and spam folder. Codes last 60 minutes. Resend becomes available after 60 seconds; resending a still-valid code keeps that code and refreshes its expiry.

Why can’t a friend see my status?

Save each other as contacts and review Profile & privacy. Statuses expire after 24 hours and are hidden between blocked accounts.

How do I turn on phone notifications?

In GOLDCHAT, open Features → Settings & accessibility → Phone permissions. Enable notifications. Also check Android App info → Notifications. Camera and microphone access may be managed through Chrome’s site settings.

Is this the messaging app?

This website explains GOLDCHAT. Choose Open app or Start chatting to sign in to the messaging app.
