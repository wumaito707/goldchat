PAYMENT QUESTIONS

Before you try again...

My bank was charged but I did not receive confirmation.

Do not pay a second time immediately. Save the reference, then contact GOLDCHAT to verify the transaction.

What if hosted checkout is not available?

Do not use an unexpected payment link. Contact GOLDCHAT to discuss the agreed payment method and wait for verified instructions.

How can I request a cancellation or refund?

Contact GOLDCHAT with your campaign agreement and payment reference. Cancellation and refund terms should be agreed in writing before payment.
